## Notable Span: PII Redaction

The input message contained an email address which was redacted before logging:

**Original input:** "What is your refund policy? My email is user@example.com"

**Logged value:** "What is your refund policy? My email is [REDACTED_EMAIL]"

This demonstrates:
1. PII is detected before logging
2. Original PII never appears in logs.jsonl
3. Redaction preserves context for debugging
